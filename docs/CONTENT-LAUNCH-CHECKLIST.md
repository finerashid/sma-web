# SMA website content launch checklist

Do not publish regulatory, affiliation, intake, eligibility or fee claims until
they are supported by a current written source.

## Mandatory confirmation

- Current official college name and approved spelling
- Legal or operational relationship between the college, “Sumitra Memorial” and the attached hospital
- University affiliation for the applicable academic year
- Approval or recognition documents and competent authority
- BNYS annual intake
- BNYS syllabus and course duration
- NEET, counselling and other entrance requirements
- Class 12 subject and percentage requirements
- Complete fee structure, payment schedule and refund policy
- Admission dates and required documents
- Current leadership and faculty roster with approved credentials
- Resolve the current website’s duplicate “President” designation for Dr. Mohan Sharma and Dr. Azeem Khan
- Current contact number, email, office hours and map location
- Hostel availability and charges
- Public yoga programme schedule and registration method
- Current naturopathy-clinic service availability and access method
- Digital learning-centre system count and published 9am–6pm access hours
- Whether PMKVY is still an active offering; if so, obtain its approved title, duration, eligibility and intake

## Content assets

- Current college logo in SVG or transparent PNG
- Approved hero photograph at least 1800 px wide
- Faculty portraits with names and designations
- Gallery images grouped into meaningful folders
- Current prospectus PDF
- Affiliation and approval PDFs
- Fee and refund documents
- Anti-ragging, grievance and privacy contacts

## Editorial checks

- Use `BNYS`, never `BYNS`
- Avoid “doctor without NEET” and similar promotional claims
- Do not say “equivalent to any medical degree” without exact official wording
- Add effective dates to time-sensitive notices
- Remove obsolete notices instead of silently editing their dates
- Confirm consent before publishing identifiable student photographs
